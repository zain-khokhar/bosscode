# Non-Tech Visual Conversion & "Every Page Is a Landing Page" Skill

Use this skill whenever creating, editing, or optimizing pages, sections, or copy for BossCode's non-technical business audience (shopkeepers, clinics, wholesalers, workshops, and WhatsApp sellers).

## 1. Brand POV & Core Positioning

- **Who We Are:** A Karachi-based team helping Pakistani businesses move **From Paper (Registers, Diaries, Parchi, Messy Chats) → To Simple Digital Software & Automation**.
- **What We Sell:**
  1. **Ready-to-Use Business Systems:** `ShopLedger` (Accounts, Stock & Udhaar), `ClinicFlow` (Appointments & Patient Files), `BillBot` (Auto Billing & Payment Reminders), `OrderTrack` (Orders & Staff Tasks), `ChatPilot` (WhatsApp Auto-Replies & Confirmations).
  2. **Custom Digitalization & Automation:** Tailored tools built around how the business already works.
- **Target Buyer Psychology:**
  - Non-technical business owners do **not** read paragraphs or care about tech stacks.
  - They care about **3 outcomes:** *Saving time*, *Stopping money/stock leaks (udhaar/missing stock)*, and *Ease of use for staff ("Can my counter boy learn it in 1 day?")*.
  - They trust **visual proof** (seeing `Rs 84,500` sales, `3 left` stock warnings, WhatsApp reminder previews) and **low-risk human contact** (`Free first chat on WhatsApp`, `You own your data`, `Support after launch`).

## 2. The "Every Page Is a Landing Page" Architecture

Users land directly on any URL via WhatsApp shares, Google, or referrals. Every page must follow the **3-Second Visual Hook → Interactive Micro-UI → 1-Tap Action** structure:

1. **Instant Orientation (Top 30% of Viewport):**
   - Eyebrow badge (2–3 words) + High-contrast H1 (4–7 words) + 1-sentence plain-language lead (max 15 words).
2. **Show, Don't Tell (Visual Anchor):**
   - Never explain a feature with a paragraph if a **Micro-UI widget**, **Before/After toggle**, or **Live ProductMock** can show it visually in 2 seconds.
3. **Micro-UI Cards over Paragraphs:**
   - Replace multi-sentence cards with: `[Icon] + [Bold 3-Word Title] + [1-Line Outcome (max 10 words)] + [Visual Tag/Link]`.
4. **Frictionless Conversion Bridge:**
   - Every page must offer a 1-tap path to WhatsApp (`Chat on WhatsApp`) or the 30-second Contact selector.
